Winning Patterns, Payouts, and Rules

Bingo Procedures


Of Legal Age
Players must be at least 21 years of age to play.
Card Ownership:
Cards are valid only for the specific game or session in which they were purchased.
Visible Marking:
Players are responsible for clearly marking (daubing) their own cards. Standard bingo daubers, markers, or chips must be used.

Claiming Bingo

Loud and Proud
Announce your Bingo immediately to stop play. You must call Bingo while the winning number is the current number called. Late calls will not be paid.
Multiple Winners
Multiple calls of bingo on the same game will be paid via split pot.
"Crazy" Games
Crazy patters are valid in any configuration that is matching the pattern for the game. Example: Crazy Kite may be in any of the 4 corners, as long as it is a complete configuration.

Extra Games

  • Lucky Sevens: $5.00 each
  • Progressive Coverall: $5.00 each
  • Speed (Blackout by #41): $8.00 each
